# What works today

[What you can change](what-you-can-change.md) is the wish list. This page says how much of it is built.

Every setting has a default. The exact names and choices are in [settings.md](settings.md).

## Built

**How deep a choice goes.** All five levels: the whole video, one section, one mystery, one kind of prayer, one bead. The narrowest wins. In the app, pick what a choice covers in the bar at the top of Voice, Words, Colors and Pace.

**The beads**

- The five-decade rosary, 59 beads, drawn as in the first video.
- The medal: plain, St. Benedict, Miraculous.
- What hangs at the end: Latin crucifix, St. Benedict crucifix, Orthodox three-bar cross, Greek cross, Celtic cross, San Damiano cross, knotted cross, tassel, a medal.
- Material: plain outline, wood, glass, stone, pearl, metal, knots of wool. Color and size.
- Chain or cord, and its color.
- Which way around the loop you go.
- A prayer said on the chain lights that stretch of chain, between its two beads. Only a bead gets a ring.

**The prayers**

- The rosary, with all four sets of mysteries, or the set for the day.
- Your own five meditations.
- The whole thing, one decade, or any run of decades. With or without the opening and the closing.
- O My Jesus and the Closing Prayer on or off. Both are labelled optional on screen.
- The Glory Be on the chain or on the next big bead.
- Your own wording for any prayer.
- Extra prayers at the start or the end.
- A reading before each mystery. You supply the text.
- A pause to think after each mystery is named.

**The voice**

- Any voice in your ElevenLabs account, by name. The tool fetches the sound itself.
- A different voice at any of the five levels.
- Two voices that take turns on the Our Father, Hail Mary and Glory Be.
- Several voices that take turns leading: a new one on every prayer, or one for each decade.
- Several recordings ("takes") of each prayer that take turns, so repeats sound less alike. Or a new recording every time a prayer is said.
- In turn, or mixed up.
- Record one bead again, when a recording came out wrong.
- Your own recordings in place of a generated voice.
- Speed. 1 is the pace of the first video.
- Pauses before and after each prayer.
- A voice that drops out after the first few Hail Marys of each decade.
- No voice at all.
- Background music or chant that you supply.
- Every recording is made once and kept. Nothing spends credits without a yes that names the number.

**The pictures**

- A picture for each mystery, the opening, the closing, the title and the ending.
- Inside the loop, above the words, or filling the background.
- Which part shows, and how close.
- The credit line, on by default.
- No pictures: the loop holds the mystery's name instead.

**The words on screen**

- Size.
- Show the prayer always, only the first time, or never.
- Follow along: the line being spoken lights up.
- The bead counter, the progress line, the mystery name, hints, the "Optional" label. Each on or off.

**The screen**

- Nine named sizes, from the Pixel 9 Pro Fold to a 4K TV. Or any exact size.
- Wide screens put the beads beside the words. Tall screens put the beads on top.
- Dark or light. Every color. Fonts.
- Safe edges.

**The pace**

- How long each bead stays up with no voice.
- The pauses, and whether they differ a little from bead to bead.
- A soft fade or a plain cut between beads.
- How long the title and the ending last.

**What comes out**

- A video file.
- A sound-only file.
- Chapters.
- Captions as a separate file.
- The tap-through guide.

**Saved setups**

- Save a setup under a name and use it again.
- Start from a saved setup or a preset and change one thing.
- Share a setup as a file.
- Seventeen presets. The first is the Luminous video as it was made.

## Not built yet

- **Other kinds of beads.** One-decade rosaries, rosary rings, prayer ropes, Anglican beads and the rest. Each needs its layout and prayers checked against a source its tradition trusts first.
- **Your own layout.** Describing beads that are not on the list.
- **Other devotions**, like the Divine Mercy Chaplet.
- **Other wordings ready to pick**: modern English, Latin, other languages. You can type your own wording today.
- **A voice language separate from the screen language.** Today the voice says what is on screen.
- **Slow movement across a picture.** Pictures are still.
- **One video file for each set of mysteries in one go.** Make them one at a time.

## Not checked yet

- **A real ElevenLabs voice.** The voice path was tested against a stand-in service, not a real account. The first real video is the test. Listen to it before trusting it.
- **How different two takes sound.** ElevenLabs reads the same words a little differently each time it is asked. The stand-in does not, so takes were checked by count, not by ear. Try a few takes of the Hail Mary before paying for more.
- **macOS and Linux.** Built and tested on Windows only.
