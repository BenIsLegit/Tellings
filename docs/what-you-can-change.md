# What you can change

This is the list of everything I want to be able to change. It says what, not how.

Every item has a default. You only touch the ones you want different.

## How deep a choice goes

Any choice below can be set at five levels. The narrowest one wins.

1. **The whole video**
2. **One section:** the opening, a decade, the closing
3. **One mystery**
4. **One kind of prayer:** every Our Father, every Hail Mary
5. **One single bead**

An example with voices:

- One voice for the whole video.
- A second voice for every Our Father.
- A third voice for the whole third mystery.
- A different voice on the very last bead.

Setting any one of those should take seconds and should not disturb the others.

## The beads

- **Which kind.** See [prayer-beads.md](prayer-beads.md).
- **Your own layout.** Any number of groups, any number of beads in a group, with or without divider beads, with or without a strand leading to the cross.
- **The strand by the cross.** How many beads it has, or none.
- **The look.** Color, size and material: wood, glass, stone, pearl, metal, or knots of wool.
- **The cord or chain.** Color and style.
- **The centerpiece.** A St. Benedict medal, a Miraculous Medal, a plain medal, a bead, or none.
- **Direction.** Which way around the loop you go.

## The cross or ending

- Latin crucifix
- St. Benedict crucifix
- Orthodox three-bar cross
- Greek cross, with equal arms
- Knotted cross, as on a prayer rope
- Tassel
- Celtic cross
- San Damiano cross
- A medal instead of a cross
- None

## The prayers

- **Which devotion.** The rosary, the Divine Mercy Chaplet, the Jesus Prayer on a prayer rope, and others as each kind of bead is added.
- **Which mysteries.** Pick a set, let the day of the week pick it, or write your own meditations.
- **How much.** The whole thing, one decade, or any stretch in between.
- **Wording.** Traditional English with "thee" and "thy", modern English, Latin, or another language.
- **Your own text** for any prayer.
- **Optional prayers** on or off: O My Jesus, the Closing Prayer, and any extra prayers you add at the start or end.
- **The opening.** Start at the crucifix, or go straight to the first decade.
- **Where the Glory Be goes.** On the chain, or on the next big bead.
- **A Scripture reading** before each mystery, quoted word for word.
- **A pause to think** after each mystery is named.

## The voice

- **Any voice** in your ElevenLabs account: one you cloned, one you designed, or one from its library.
- **A different voice** at any of the five levels above.
- **Which ElevenLabs model speaks**, and how: steadier or freer, plain or with direction like "reverent".
- **Two voices that take turns.** One leads the first half of each prayer and the other answers with the second half, the way a group prays.
- **Your own recordings** in place of a generated voice.
- **Speed.**
- **Pauses** between prayers.
- **The language** the voice speaks.
- **A voice that drops out.** It says the first few Hail Marys of a decade, then leaves you to say the rest.
- **No voice at all.** Words on screen only.
- **Background sound.** Music or chant that you supply, or silence.

## The pictures

- **A picture for each mystery or section.** A classical painting, an icon, or your own image.
- **Where it sits.** Inside the loop of beads, beside it, or filling the background.
- **Which part shows.** The crop, the zoom, and what is centered.
- **Slow movement** across the picture, or a still.
- **The credit line.** Artist, title and date. On by default.
- **Pictures for the opening and closing**, or text there instead.
- **No pictures.**

## The words on screen

- **Size.**
- **Show or hide the prayer text.** Always, never, or only the first time each prayer comes up.
- **Follow along.** The line being spoken stands out.
- **The bead counter.** Ten dots that fill as you go through a decade.
- **The progress line.** "Prayer 13 of 75" and the bar under it.
- **The mystery name and number.**
- **Hints.** Lines like "No bead for this one. Hold the chain."
- **Language**, separate from the voice. Latin spoken with English on screen, for example.

## The screen

- **Presets** for phones, folding phones, tablets and TVs. The first is the Pixel 9 Pro Fold inner screen, 2076 x 2152.
- **Wide, tall or square**, or any exact size.
- **Dark or light.**
- **Colors and fonts.**
- **Safe edges**, so nothing sits under a camera cutout or rounded corner.

## The pace

- How long each bead stays up when there is no voice
- How long the pause is after each prayer
- How one bead changes to the next: a cut or a soft fade
- How long the opening title and the ending last

## What comes out

- **A video file** for each set of mysteries, or just the one you ask for.
- **A sound-only version**, for praying on a walk with the beads in your hand.
- **Chapters**, so you can jump to a decade.
- **Captions** as a separate file.
- **The tap-through guide**, which moves a bead at a time at your own pace.

## Saved setups

- Save a whole setup under a name and reuse it.
- Start a new video from a saved setup and change one thing.
- Share a setup with someone else.
- The first saved setup should be the Luminous video as it was made. It is described in [`prototype/README.md`](../prototype/README.md).
