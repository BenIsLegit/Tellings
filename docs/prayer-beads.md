# Prayer beads

The tool should draw the beads a person owns. This is the list of what people own.

**How far to trust this page.** Only the first entry has been checked against sources and built. The rest come from general knowledge. Before building any of them, check the layout and the prayers against a source that tradition itself trusts.

## Checked and built

### Five-decade rosary

The standard Catholic rosary. 59 beads.

- A crucifix
- Five beads above it: one big, three small, one big
- A medal where the strand joins the loop
- Five sets of ten small beads, with one big bead between sets

A **St. Benedict rosary** is this same rosary with a St. Benedict medal and a St. Benedict cross.

## To check, then build

### Smaller rosaries

- **One-decade rosary.** One big bead, ten small beads and a cross. Often carried in a pocket.
- **Rosary ring.** A ring with ten bumps and a cross, turned on the finger.
- **Rosary bracelet.** One decade worn on the wrist.

These have no strand of beads above the cross. That is one reason guides don't match each other.

### Larger rosaries

- **Fifteen-decade and twenty-decade rosaries.** All the sets of mysteries on one long loop.

### Other Catholic beads

- **Franciscan Crown.** Seven decades, for the Seven Joys of Mary.
- **Servite rosary.** Seven groups of seven beads, for the Seven Sorrows of Mary.
- **Brigittine rosary.** Six decades.
- **Chaplets with their own beads.** There are many, each with its own count. Add them one at a time as they are wanted.

### Other prayers on a standard rosary

- **Divine Mercy Chaplet.** Prayed on ordinary five-decade rosary beads, with its own prayers.

Same beads, different words. The beads and the prayers need to be separate choices.

### Orthodox prayer rope

Called a komboskini in Greek and a chotki in Russian.

- Knots of wool, not beads. Common sizes are 33, 50 and 100 knots.
- Some have a divider bead every so many knots.
- It ends in a knotted cross, often with a tassel.
- The usual prayer is the Jesus Prayer, once on each knot: "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner."

There are no mysteries and no sets. The video for a rope is much simpler than the video for a rosary, and the tool should let it be simple.

### Anglican prayer beads

33 beads.

- A cross
- One bead above it
- Four large beads that divide the loop into quarters
- Four groups of seven small beads between them

### Anything else

A person should be able to describe beads that are not on this list: how many groups, how many in each, what divides them, and what hangs at the end.

## Crosses and endings

What hangs at the end differs as much as the beads do. The tool should offer each of these with any kind of beads:

- Latin crucifix
- St. Benedict crucifix
- Orthodox three-bar cross
- Greek cross, with equal arms
- Knotted cross
- Tassel
- Celtic cross
- San Damiano cross
- A medal
