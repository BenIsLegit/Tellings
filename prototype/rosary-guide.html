Mysteries

The whole rosary in five lines

  • Crucifix. Sign of the Cross, then the Apostles' Creed.
  • Big bead. Our Father.
  • Small bead. Hail Mary.
  • Chain after a set of small beads. Glory Be. Many people add the O My Jesus prayer.
  • Medal, at the very end. Hail, Holy Queen.

Why other guides look different

A standard rosary has five beads between the crucifix and the medal: one big, three small, one big. A St. Benedict rosary is a standard rosary with a St. Benedict medal and cross. Count yours. If you find those five beads and then five sets of ten, this guide matches your beads.

A guide that shows no beads there is likely showing a one-decade rosary or a rosary ring, or it starts straight at the first decade. A different count usually means a chaplet, which is a different prayer said on different beads.

Guides also differ on small things, like whether the Glory Be goes on the chain or on the next big bead. Pick one way and stay with it.

If it feels like too much

Lost your place? Pick up at the nearest bead and keep going. The beads are there so you do not have to count.

One decade is a fine place to start. That is one Our Father, ten Hail Marys and one Glory Be.
